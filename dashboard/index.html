TOKEN RADAR

Pre-token protocols, ranked by what a token would be worth if each protocol's record stayed as it is — and how to farm each one before it issues.

How a row is priced

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × maturity factor

Peer multiple

The market's own price of a TVL dollar. For each category, take the protocols whose token already trades and divide market cap by TVL; the multiple is that category's median ratio (needs at least 4 peers, else the market-wide median). Clamped to 0.02–2.50 so no thin category runs away.

Growth factor

1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%) × 0.5. A protocol adding TVL this week is priced up; one bleeding TVL is priced down, at half weight.

Maturity factor

0.60 rising to 1.00 over the first 1,095 days DefiLlama has tracked the protocol. A launch priced off three years of record is worth more than the same TVL priced off a month of it.

Who is ranked

Every protocol the snapshot's watchlist names, plus every other protocol that records no token and is not a product of one that trades (no exchange products, bridge or staking receipts, versioned pools). Protocols whose token already trades are never ranked — they sit in Already live at their real market caps, and they are the peers the multiples come from.

Every figure comes from the sealed snapshot; no name is priced by hand, and each row shows its own arithmetic. An implied value is this board's claim about a token that does not exist yet — the snapshot says a protocol records no token, which is DefiLlama's record, not proof none exists.
